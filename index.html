DAhYO Help

Basics

＋ Track creates audio or aux tracks, mono or stereo — imported beats land on stereo automatically. Select a track and hit R to arm it (●), then the record button to roll. Space plays/stops, Enter plays from the start, L loops. Every channel has Tune → EQ → Comp → Delay → Reverb; save any vocal chain as a template and apply it anywhere. Sessions autosave in this browser (audio included); export .dahyo files to move work between devices.

Timeline tips

Click a clip to select it, drag it to move it, drag its amber edge handles for fade in/out. ⚑ adds a marker at the playhead — click it to jump, double-click to rename, right-click to delete. ⧉ on a track header or mixer strip duplicates the track with everything (clips, FX, routing, fader/pan).

Browser support

The full DAW — recording, mixing, effects, WAV export, sessions — works in Chrome, Edge, Safari and Firefox on Windows and Mac.

Output-device switching needs Chrome or Edge (it requires setSinkId) — Safari and Firefox use the system default output.

Save-folder auto-save (.dahyo files on your device) needs Chrome or Edge (File System Access API) — everywhere else sessions stay in the browser and you can still export/import .dahyo files by hand.

Recording needs microphone permission; Safari records takes as mp4 instead of webm — handled automatically.

Pitch correction (Tune) needs AudioWorklet, supported in all four modern browsers — if a browser lacks it, the Tune effect is bypassed and labeled, and everything else keeps working.

Where should DAhYO keep your sessions?

DAhYO can auto-save every session as a .dahyo file inside a DAhYO Sessions folder on this device — real files you can copy, back up, or move to another computer. Nothing is written anywhere until you choose.

You can change this anytime in Sessions. Vocal chain templates are saved as files too.
